# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 July 2026_

This is a plain-language explanation of how Hammer handles your data, from Dark Rock Studios, the
makers of Hammer. The short version: Hammer is offline-first, your writing lives on your own
devices, and we don't collect anything about you automatically. The longer version is below.

## 1. Your writing stays on your devices

Everything you create in Hammer — your stories, notes, encyclopedia entries, and settings — is saved
as ordinary files on your own device. We can't see any of it. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else,
and you don't need an account to use the app.

## 2. We don't track you

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no ads. We don't collect information about how you
use the app, and we're not interested in it. The only thing the app reaches out to the internet for
on its own is to check whether a newer version is available.

## 3. Sync is optional

Hammer can sync your work between your devices, but only if you turn it on — it's off by default.
When you do, you connect the app to a sync server, and the content you choose to sync, along with
your account details, is sent to that server so your devices can stay in step. How that server
handles your data is up to whoever operates it; if it's a server someone else runs, their privacy
policy applies, not this one.

## 4. Bug reports are yours to send

If something breaks and you want to report it, the app can gather recent logs to help us understand
what went wrong. You decide whether to send them, and you can see what's included first. Nothing
leaves your device unless you choose to send it.

## 5. Security

For anything that does leave your device through a feature you turned on, we do our best to protect
it. That said, no method of sending data over the internet is ever completely secure, and we won't
pretend otherwise.

## 6. Links to other places

Hammer may occasionally link to other websites. Those aren't ours, and we can't speak for how they
handle your data. If you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

## 7. Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared
personal information through Hammer, get in touch and we'll address it. You also need to be at least
16 to consent to the processing of your personal information yourself (in some countries a parent or
guardian can do so on your behalf).

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at <darkrockstudios@gmail.com>.

Thanks for using Hammer, and happy writing.
